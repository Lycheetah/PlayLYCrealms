How to play

Four steps from here to your first night.

  1. Type your code at the top and press Play. The game opens in this tab.
  2. Start in Survival. A camp waits, and First Light walks you through your first night.
  3. Turn on sound under Play, and raise the graphics in Settings if your device can take it.
  4. Stuck? Press B for the handbook, or ask Lychee AI on the in-game phone.

Updated often

What's new

  • 🧭 Exploration, made whole: The map is yours to uncover: it stays dark until you have walked there, and the landmarks in the dark stay hidden. Hold the Spyglass and look at a landmark far off (out to 450 blocks): it is named, marked on your compass and uncovered on your map. Places now count when you walk into them, not when they appear near you, and the Atlas follows it: Cartographer for 150 squares of map, Far Sight for 10 landmarks spotted, and the title Atlas-Bearer for 50 places entered. The Field Guide's Exploration & Adventure page holds the whole line, with the Spyglass, the Star Chart and the Cartographer's Table.
  • 🚗 Vehicles, made whole: Everything you ride, drive, fly or sail now lives on one Field Guide page, Vehicles & Mounts, in five tiers: ride, wheels and rails, engines, sky, beyond. New: saddles. A Leather Saddle (four hides and an iron) makes every mount of that kind 15% faster; a Racing Saddle (a Leather Saddle, two gold and a feather) 30% faster, with a higher jump. Hold one and right click near your horse or any mount you have tamed. And the Garage (six iron, four planks and a circuit), the workshop for all of it: right click it to open the Shipwright, Shift + right click to call your craft to it.
  • 🐉 Two new legendary sets: The Hollowsun Dragonlord: obsidian scale veined with magma, four armour pieces, Sunfang the Dragonlord Greatsword (right click: Sunbreak, a 12 block wave of solar fire) and a Hollowsun Wyrmling Egg, forged from Sunscale and Wyrm Hearts. And the Glacial Sovereign: four armour pieces, Rimebane the Winter Runeblade and a Frost Colossus Egg, forged from Winter Hearts. Find them in Creative → 🆕 New & experimental, or forge them at the bench.
  • 🌅 The sun put right: Facing the sun used to wash the whole screen white: the shafts of sunlight took their light from the entire sky, and the glow lit anything bright, a blue sky included. Now the shafts come only from the sun, through the gaps in trees and hills, and the glow only from what is truly bright: the sun, lava, lamps. And the graphics levels mean something: High adds sun shadows with cool shaded sides, finer shafts and a softer glow, and sees 10 chunks; Ultra wider shadows and every light, 12 chunks; Max soft shadows and the finest light, 14 chunks. Choosing a level brings its view distance with it (you can still change it). Also: building a kit no longer refuses because a Delve or a road happens to be going up nearby.
Earlier updates
  • 🍲 Farming, made whole: Farming is the first part of the game made into one clear line, from the first seed to the kitchen. A Watering Can (three iron at the bench) fills at water and waters your sprouts: watered crops grow twice as fast. A Cooking Pot (four iron and a bucket) set down beside a Bonfire, Brazier or Kiln is a new station in your Craft list, with eight meals, each made from your crops and each with an effect for three minutes: Hearty Stew heals you slowly, Sun Melon Juice makes you swift, Ember Chili makes you mine faster, Lumen Soup gives you night eyes, Fish Pie doubles your air on the Moon, Rose Hip Tea keeps the wild calm, Lychee Sorbet lightens your step, and Harvest Bread keeps you full. One meal at a time; the timer shows above your hearts. The Field Guide's Farming page now holds the whole line, with the Seed Cannon, the Farmer's Plot and the Growth Sigil brought home to it.
  • 🔧 Every break fixed: We read through four whole parts of the game, farming, vehicles, exploration and space, and fixed everything we found broken. Crops planted by a Farmer's Plot, the Seed Cannon, a farm kit or a sky garden now grow (they never did, and old ones near you will start); the Seed Cannon plants the seeds it fires, not always wheat; apiaries count every flower; and every fish you catch can be cooked. Aboard a craft, Y opens the Shipwright again (on foot it is still the Travel Map). Microchips and Plasma Cores can be made, so can the Jump Drive to the Moon; so can the Star Chart. Finishing the Road to the PILLAR gives you the title Pillar-Called at last, opened vaults and cleared Delves leave your compass, and the Hermit points at real raiders, not at his own hut.

Found a bug? In the game: Menu → ✉ Feedback → Copy report, then send it to Lycheetah. This is an early build of a big game, and every report helps.

🪨Stone tools

🔥A Bonfire
  • Your hands are a tool. Hold the button on a block to break it. A tree trunk gives logs even bare-handed, just slowly. Stone needs a pick. Coal Ore takes any pick; copper, tin and iron ore need a Stone Pick or better.
  • Hand, bench and kiln. Open your bag (E) and Craft: a log makes four planks, two planks make four hafts, four planks make a Maker's Bench. Bench recipes work while the bench is in your bag or within 4 blocks. A Kiln only works set down near you.
  • Fire. Four logs and four stone make a Bonfire by hand. It burns forever, and standing within 4 blocks of it heals you. Right click it with raw meat or fish to cook. Torches take a haft and coal or charcoal.
  • Food keeps you healing. Your 🍗 bar drains as you move, faster when you sprint. Health only comes back while the bar is above three drumsticks, and faster when it is nearly full. Empty, you slowly starve down to two hearts.
  • Living off the land. Break grass and ferns for plant fibre; three twist into cord. Gravel sometimes gives flint. Two hafts, cord and flint make a Fire Drill, which lights a bonfire anywhere for two logs. Four vines and cord make a Vine Swing.

Start here: Hold the button on a tree trunk until you have a few logs, then open your bag (E) → Craft and make planks, hafts and a Maker's Bench.

🧱Ingots, then alloys
  • The right pick. Every ore asks for a pick of a certain metal or better. Coal takes any pick; copper, tin and iron need stone; gold and silver need iron; obsidian needs Embersteel. An item's page in this guide names the pick it needs.
  • Smelting. A Kiln within 4 blocks of you smelts with fuel (coal, charcoal or sulfur dust): two lode make two ingots. Later, a Bellows Kiln on 10 ⟳ or an Electric Furnace on 6 ⚡ smelts with no fuel at all.
  • Grind first, get double. A Millstone or Crusher grinds one lode into two dust, and two dust smelt into two ingots. So grinding before you smelt doubles most metals. A Crusher sometimes throws in an extra dust.
  • Alloys. All in a Kiln: three copper and one tin make four Bronze; one iron and two coal make Steel; two copper and one zinc make three Brass. Each far realm adds its own metal, such as Embersteel, Moonsilver and Rosegold.
  • Finding ore. The Ore Finder names the best ore within 16 blocks, how far away it is and which way. Your Mining skill grows as you dig and makes every pick quicker, up to a quarter faster.

Start here: Make a Stone Pick at the Maker's Bench (3 stone, 2 hafts), then a Kiln (8 stone) and set it down beside your bench.

🍞Harvest, bake
  • Seeds and soil. Breaking tall grass sometimes gives Wheat Seeds. A Hoe turns grass or dirt into Tilled Soil; plant seeds on it. A ripe crop gives one or two of its harvest and one or two seeds back.
  • How fast it grows. A crop ripens in two minutes, or in one if water lies within 4 blocks, level with the soil, when you plant it. Growth runs on the clock, so your fields keep growing while you are away.
  • Seasons. In Mundus, Rubedo and Viriditas the season turns every seven days. Spring and summer grow a quarter faster, autumn a little slower, winter much slower. The other realms grow at one steady pace.
  • Hurry it along. Compost (four plant fibre and a dirt, by hand) ripens one young crop on the spot. Ring a Growth Bell and every young crop within 9 blocks ripens; it rests a minute after. A charged Growth Sigil makes crops near it grow three times as fast.
  • Farms that tend themselves. Set an ally to Farm here and it harvests ripe crops within 8 blocks and replants them. Every harvest also trains your Farming skill, which raises the chance of an extra crop, up to one in three.

Start here: Make a Hoe at the Maker's Bench (2 planks, 2 hafts), break tall grass for seeds, and till a strip of soil right beside water.

💥Combo finisher
  • Armour. Wear a head, body, legs and feet piece. The body counts most, then legs, head and feet. Together armour can cut harm by up to four fifths. A Shield held up (right mouse) stops three quarters of a hit from the front.
  • Read the warning. Before a creature's signature move, a glowing mark on the ground shows where it will land and fills as it comes. Dash out with Q: you cannot be hit while dashing. Hit a common creature as it winds up to break the move.
  • Combos. Hits chain into a string: three with a sword, two with a heavy weapon. The last is a finisher that launches the foe. Dash to cancel a swing; dash through a hit as it lands for a perfect dodge and a double-damage counter.
  • Healing. Health starts coming back on its own seven seconds after you were last hit, but only while your food bar is above three drumsticks. Standing within 4 blocks of a Bonfire or Brazier heals you too.
  • Practice. Menu → 🥋 Combat Trial raises a floating arena where a coach teaches every move in about two minutes. Menu → ⚖ Difficulty sets how hard this world hits you.

Start here: Make a Stone Sword at the Maker's Bench (2 stone, 1 haft), then open Menu → 🥋 Combat Trial.

💡Lamp on charge
  • Fire you carry. A haft and a piece of coal or charcoal make four torches by hand. Charcoal comes from a Kiln: three logs make two. Holding a torch or a lantern lights the way around you.
  • Fires that heal. A Bonfire (four logs and four stone, by hand) burns forever. Stand within 4 blocks of a Bonfire or a Brazier and you heal. Right click either one with raw meat or fish to cook it.

📦Chest beside it
  • Turning power ⟳. A Water Wheel turns 1.2 ⟳ for each water block around it, up to 16, and half again in a river. A Windmill wants open air and height: up to 16 ⟳. A Lava Engine takes 6 ⟳ per lava block it touches.
  • One network, one speed. Everything touching, directly or through Gearboxes, shares one supply. A Millstone wants 6 ⟳, a Press 8, a Bellows Kiln 10. When the network makes less than its machines want, they all slow down together. A Battery Box stores spare turning for later.
  • Charge ⚡ is separate. Charge travels only through Cable, or wire strung between Wire Connectors (copper reaches 12 blocks, steel 40). A Dynamo on 8 ⟳ makes 10 ⚡, a Combustion Generator burns coal for 12 ⚡, a Photovoltaic Cell drinks daylight. A Charge Bank keeps the spare.
  • Chests do the carrying. A chest on top of a machine feeds it; a chest beside or under it catches what it makes. Without one, a machine holds 64 of its output and stops until you take it. Open a machine to fit modules: a Hopper Box holds 256.
  • Why has it stopped?. Look at any machine: the line under your crosshair says what it is doing or what it needs. Stopped machines puff red sparks, short ones amber. Menu → 📱 Phone → Factory lists every stuck machine in the realm, nearest first.

Start here: Make a Water Wheel at the Maker's Bench (8 planks, 2 Wooden Gears, 4 hafts), set it in water and put a Millstone touching it; or press K and build 🎓 Lessons → Turning power.

CircuitsheetLever → Signal Wire → Inverter or gate → Lamp with charge
🎚️Lever
➜
〰️Signal Wire
➜
🔁Inverter or gate
➜
💡Lamp with charge
  • A signal is a switch, not power. Levers, Buttons, Pressure Plates, Daylight Sensors and Pulse Timers make a signal; Signal Wire carries it and glows when it is on. Anything touching the wire (a lamp, machine, turret or door) runs only while the signal is on. Lamps still need their 1 ⚡.
  • Sources. A Lever stays as you flip it. A Button is on for a moment. A Pressure Plate is on while something stands on it. A Daylight Sensor is on by day. A Pulse Timer blinks every 1, 2, 4 or 8 seconds: right click to change it.
  • Gates have a front. A gate reads its back and both sides and sends its answer out of its front: the side that faced you when you set it down. An Inverter turns one signal over. AND wants every input on, OR any one, XOR an odd number.
  • Memory and timing. A Memory Cell flips each time a pulse reaches its back; a signal on a side clears it. A Signal Counter pulses once every 2 to 16 pulses (right click to set). A Sequencer runs eight steps you choose, on its own clock if its back is empty.
  • Seeing what it does. Hold a Signal Probe to read any wire or gate. A Number Display shows the count, step or memory of a Counter, Sequencer or Memory Cell touching it. A Keypad sends a signal when someone enters its four-digit code.

Start here: Press K and build 🎓 Lessons → Signals, or make a Lever by hand (a haft and a stone) and run Signal Wire from it to a lamp that has charge.

⚙️Machine or chest
  • Chests beside machines. The first automation needs no tubes at all: a chest on top of a machine feeds it, and a chest beside or under it catches what it makes. A chest holds 27 different kinds of thing.
  • Tubes need a pump. A tube line starts with a Pump Tube. It pulls one item a second out of the chest or machine it touches and sends it to the nearest machine that can use it, or else to a chest with room.
  • Sorting. Hold an item and right click a Sorting Tube: from then on only that item passes it. Put a sorter at the start of each branch and every kind finds its own way.
  • Belts. A Conveyor Belt runs the way you faced when you set it. It takes an item a second from a chest or machine behind it, carries two at a time, drops them into whatever is in front, and carries you too.

Start here: Set a chest on top of a machine and another beside it; then press K and build 🎓 Lessons → Tubes.

🧠Thinking Core
  • Build one, teach it. Hold an Automaton Kit and right click to set down a clockwork helper. Hold a Rule Card (Dig, Chop, Haul or Mend) and right click the helper to teach it. Right click it with an empty hand to choose its job.

Start here: Make an Automaton Kit at the Maker's Bench (4 Iron Gears, 4 Steel Sheets, 2 Circuits), set it down, then teach it a Rule Card: Chop.

🏡Care Pen
  • Taming. Hold the food a creature likes and right click it until the hearts fill: a sheep wants 2 wheat, a horse 3 wheat or apples, a wolf 3 meat, a lion 5. Sheep, chickens and goats join your herd.
  • Tame creatures. Right click a tame creature with an empty hand. Herd animals switch between staying and following. Pets, allies and mounts open a sheet: Follow, Stay or Guard here, and allies can also Farm.

Every system in the game

Open an area to see what you can do. Everything here works in the game today.

🌍Realms and the worldSeven home realms, and eleven more beyond them
  • The seven realms. Mundus, Nigredo, Albedo, Rubedo, Viriditas, Citrinitas and Mortificatio, each with its own land and life. Walk into a Gate, or press T.
  • Places beyond. Three eras, Albedo's Moon, the Underworlds, the Aurum, the Rift, Neon City and more.
  • Weather and climate. Showers come and go, snow falls in the cold, and dry lands stay dry. About once an hour a thunderstorm rolls across the land.
  • Seasons. Each season lasts seven days and colours the land. Every fifth night is a Blood Moon.
  • A land full of places. Crashed airships, wizard towers, old mines, lost temples, bandit towers and four rare wonders. Most keep a chest, and each goes on your compass.
  • Roads and sky forts. Stone roads join the cities you find. Sky fortresses float over Mundus: climb the vine, beat the Sky Captain and break the totem.
  • The deep world. Great caverns with underground lakes, ravines, crystal geodes, ember chasms and frost grottos. Volcanoes sometimes erupt.
  • Giants on the horizon. Each open realm has something huge in the distance, like the PILLAR in Mundus, and great sky whales cross the far sky.
  • Strange days. The world does odd things: a fizzing gremlin, the Burger Baron, fairy rings, meteors and a Leprechaun with a Pot of Gold.
🔥Survival and the first hourA camp, a cottage and a night to remember
  • The camp and First Light. A new Survival world starts at a camp. First Light (top left) walks you through eight steps, from your bag to flying the Sky Raft.
  • The Hearth. In Mundus a cottage rises by where you wake, with beds, a bench, a kiln, a farm and supplies. Your home is set there.
  • The first night. Falling stars land nearby, and three waves of night creatures come for the Hearth. Hold each for a reward.
  • Hearts and falling. Ten hearts, and armour softens each blow. If you fall your bag is safe, and a ☠ marks the spot.
  • Hunger. The 🍗 bar empties as you walk and run. Hold food and right click to eat. Kept full, you heal faster.
  • Light and fire. Torches light the dark and a Bonfire burns forever, heals you while you stand by it, and cooks meat and fish.
  • Home. Set home here, then Go home from anywhere in that realm, from the 🏡 button, ⚡ Actions or the menu. You can keep a home in every realm.
🧱Building, kits and the TerraformerBlock by block, or a whole base at once
  • Break and place. Break a block to collect it, then place it anywhere. Every world keeps your changes, and Creative gives you every block and free flight.
  • The Builder's Wand. Fill, replace or clear a whole box of blocks, or select one for a blueprint. R changes mode, and Shift with right click undoes.
  • Blueprints. Save a box at the Blueprint Table by name, then lay it down anywhere behind a glowing frame. In Survival you need the blocks it uses.
  • The Kit Library. Press K for ready-made ships, homes, labs, farms and landmarks. In Survival a kit uses the blocks in your bag, and Essence covers the rest.
  • Mega kits. Eleven kits on a huge scale, from a Nomad Citadel to a Star Cruiser that can jump to the Moon. Their machine lines are wired and ready to run.
  • The Lessons shelf. Kit Library → 🎓 Lessons builds four working setups for power, charge, signals and tubes, with signs and a checklist that fills itself in.
  • Kits that teach and travel. A card explains each kit as it goes up. Share your blueprints as a code or a file in Menu → 🧰 Kit Gallery.
  • The Pattern Stone. Right click two corners of something you built to record it, pack it in a Kit Crate, and set it down anywhere.
  • The Terraformer. A staff that raises, lowers, flattens and smooths the land. It can also capture a piece of land as a kit.
⚙️Machines and powerFrom a water wheel to a thinking factory
  • Turning power. Water Wheels, Windmills and Lava Engines turn Gearboxes that drive Millstones, Sawmills, Presses and Bellows. Right click a machine to open it.
  • The Electric Age. Generators make charge, wires hang between posts, and Charge Banks store it. It runs Electric Furnaces, Crushers and the Quarry.
  • Heavy power. Fill a Reactor with rods and coolant, and watch the heat or it melts down. Geothermal and wireless power nodes do the rest.
  • Chests that feed. A chest on top feeds a machine, and a chest beside it catches what it makes. Your first automation, no tubes needed.
  • Tubes and belts. Pump and Sorting Tubes move items, Conveyor Belts carry them, and drones fill Request Chests.
  • Signals and circuits. Levers, sensors and timers switch lamps, doors and machines. Logic gates, counters, a Sequencer and a Keypad build real circuits.
  • Machine Modules. Three slots per machine for Speed Gears, Efficiency Coils, Hopper Boxes and one core. Rare modules hide in Delves.
  • Thinking machines. Teach clockwork automatons to dig, chop, haul or mend with Rule Cards. A Vault Mind turns your chests into one search box.
  • Machines that explain themselves. Look at any machine and one line says what it is doing or why it has stopped. Stopped machines send up a red spark.
  • The Factory app. On your phone, 🏭 Factory lists every stuck or starved machine in the realm, nearest first.
⚒️Items, crafting and forgingBenches, ores, gear and legendary sets
  • Crafting. Open your bag (E) and pick Craft. A Maker's Bench or Kiln nearby, or a bench in your bag, unlocks the bigger recipes.
  • Ores and metals. Dozens of ores, from tin near the surface to ruby and jade in the deep, fired into bronze, steel and the realm metals.
  • The catalogue. Every block and item sits in a section and a group, with jump chips to find it fast. The 🆕 New shelf shows what has just arrived.
  • The Anvil. Forge weapons, tools and armour, from +1 up to a cap set by the metal. The finest gear can be forged further still.
  • Fair weapons. Each weapon kind has one strength: blades are fast, spears reach, hammers crack armour, bows hit harder from afar.
  • Legendary sets. Two or four pieces of a set give its bonus: heal in water, web your foes, raise jackal guards, or build up Heat in the Asterion Forgemaster.
  • Relic and Mythic. Legendary sets climb a ladder: Relic adds defence, and Mythic adds more, with a Mythic Shard from bosses in every piece.
  • The Fitting Room. Press I to try on any armour on a big 3D you, compare the defence and set bonus, then wear it. You can wear whole sets in one tap.
  • Your Look. Choose skin, hair, eyes and clothes. Wear the look of any armour you have collected over your real gear, or dye it.
  • Loadouts. Craft a Loadout Sigil to save your armour and hotbar into three kits and swap them in one tap. Creative also has a 🛡 Full sets tab.
⚔️CombatFast, readable and fair
  • Movement. Dash (Q) and nothing can hurt you. Air dash, ground pound, wall hop, wall slide and ledge pull-up.
  • Combos and finishers. Hits chain into combos. A finisher on the ground launches the foe for an air juggle, and one in the air spikes it down.
  • Flashy moves. Dash-cancel a swing, slide-kick at a sprint, and pounce with claws. A perfect dodge slows the world and makes your next hit count double.
  • Lock-on. Press O, the middle mouse button or 🎯 to keep a foe framed.
  • Monster families. Each family has a signature move. A glowing mark shows where it will land, so dash out. Menu → Monster families shows them all.
  • Guardians. Each realm has a guardian with its own attack patterns, from the Drowned Bell to Titan Prime. Beat it for its trophy.
  • Fair Fight. Choose Peaceful, Easy, Normal, Hard or Brutal. Fierce foes glitter gold, are twice as tough, and drop Beast Cores.
  • Skills to 100. Mining, Woodcraft, Farming, Taming, Arcana, Engineering, Flight and Combat each level from 1 to 100, with fair bonuses.
  • The Combat Trial. Menu → Combat Trial: a coach in a floating arena teaches every move in about two minutes.
✨MagicSchools, spells and the Great Work
  • The Mystery Schools. Every realm hides a school. Answer its riddle at the Codex Stand and it teaches you its spells.
  • Wands and sigils. Apprentice, Adept and Archmage wands cast ten spells using Vis. Chalk sigils make crops grow, keep monsters away or link travel points.
  • The Spell Wheel. Hold R (or tap ✨) and your spells open in a ring. Point and let go.
  • The Spell Tome. Combine a shape (Bolt, Burst, Ward, Leap) with an aspect (Ember, Frost, Life, Storm, Void): twenty spells, six at a time.
  • Essence. Your Alchemical Tablet turns Essence into more of what it has learned. An Essence Forge makes it, and spells spend it.
  • The Great Work. Each guardian holds one of seven colours. Dissolve all seven trophies in the Great Athanor to finish the Work and open the way to the Aurum.
  • The Second Work. Then climb seven levels of Ascension, with harder nights and richer rewards. Open the Seventh Gate to face every guardian at once.
  • Written realms. A running Thinking Core writes a Realm Codex from what you have done. Step through the Codex Gate into a realm made from it.
🦌CreaturesA living wild, and friends to raise
  • Wildlife. Deer, boar, bison, mammoths, sheep, otters, foxes, lions and many more, with creatures that belong to each realm.
  • Hunters and prey. Wolves hunt deer and lions hunt gazelles. When one animal bolts, its herd bolts with it. Herds raise young, and fish swim in schools.
  • The living air. Birds sing near trees, mostly at dawn and dusk. Butterflies fly by day, owls and fireflies by night, and leaves drift through the woods.
  • Realm creatures. Grave Borers hunt from under the ground, Old Titans sleep in the Titan Graves, and Void Rays swim the Rift.
  • Taming and pets. Feed a wolf raw meat or a fox berries and it follows and guards you. Pets and allies come with you through gates.
  • A mount for every realm. Horses, ash beetles, cloud rays, lions, stags, camels and bonehounds. Tame one with food and ride it with F.
  • Ranch and breeding. A Ranch Post keeps your herd and collects wool, eggs and milk. Breed two of a kind and the young take traits from each parent.
  • Care Pens. Catch a small creature in a Creature Net and raise it in a Care Pen. A bird grows into your Skywing, which is how you earn flight in Survival.
  • The Bestiary. Look at a creature through the Lens to fill in its page, with gold for every ten you study.
👑People, towns and kingdomsFrom a camp to a Crown City
  • Folk and friendship. Talk to townsfolk with right click, and give gifts with Shift and right click. At five hearts they give you a present.
  • Traders and dwarves. Every settlement has a trader. Dwarven holds keep a legend at their lectern, and the dwarves' Deep Capital waits underground.
  • Living towns. Folk work by day and sleep at night. Each town asks for three things a day and grows new houses as you help.
  • Cities and peoples. Walled cities of the Bloomfolk, the Deepsmiths and the Wind-Keepers. Bring an Elder what they ask and your kingdom learns their ways.
  • Kingdom ranks. Towns climb ten ranks, from Camp to Crown City. Big towns offer contracts, and linked towns pay tribute (Menu → Kingdoms).
  • The Market Stall. Set your own prices, and merchant caravans come by to buy. Each people pays more for what it loves.
  • Festivals. The Almanac tracks the seasons, and the first day of each is a festival. There are fireworks, a fishing derby, races and the Harvest Moon.
  • Turrets and Claim Stones. Turrets shoot what hunts you, never your pets or friends. No monster rises near a Claim Stone.
  • Companions with jobs. In Menu → Companions, each tamed creature can be a Guard, a Gatherer, a Scout or Lucky.
  • Aurelia. A city on a floating island high over Mundus. Stand in the column of light beneath it and the Sky Lift carries you up.
🧭TravelRide, glide, sail and fly
  • The Travel Map. Press Y. Walk up to a Waystone to attune it, then Step there for a little Essence, take a free Sky Route, or Cross to another realm.
  • Horses and Skywings. Whistle your horse and gallop, or call a Skywing and climb, dive and surge.
  • Gliding. Carry a Glider anywhere in your bag and hold jump while falling. Look down to dive.
  • Crafts you build. Build around a Pilot's Seat and assemble something that drives, flies or floats. The Shipwright (Menu) shows what will fly.
  • Boats and submarines. Hulls float, sails go faster in a storm, and a Ballast Tank takes you under water.
  • Ferries. Every port you visit joins the ferry list in Menu → Ferry. Sail for a few gold, or for free with a Sea Pass.
  • Time Portals. Power one and fit a Chronal Core to visit Ancient Egypt, the Modern Day or the Far Future. Each era is made fresh on every visit.
  • The Moon. Fit a Jump Drive to a ship and climb high to reach Albedo's Moon. It has low gravity and craters, and you must watch your oxygen.
  • Other doors. A Deep Lift drops you to the Underworlds, a Skytrain Pass rides to Neon City, and the Warp Keystone steps you to places you know.
🐾Story and adventureThe Lycheetah, the PILLAR and the deep places
  • The Lycheetah's Path. Press J for the Quest Log: six chapters from your first steps to the Great Work, with gold for every step.
  • The Road to the PILLAR. A campaign of eight great journeys, from the Hollow Sun to the Moon. Walk them all and the PILLAR answers you every night.
  • The Lore Codex. Menu → Lore holds thirteen cards about the Lycheetah and the broken world. Each one unlocks as you find its part of the world.
  • The Storyteller. Every world writes its own history, read at any lectern. The Storyteller sends something kind after hard times.
  • The Clockwork Reach. A Campaign Block opens a wound-down brass city. Win three Gear Hearts, solve the chimes and wind the Mainspring, alone or with a friend.
  • Rifts. A Rift Stone opens a maze of rooms that is new every time. Find the key and beat the Keeper to go deeper.
  • Delves and keys. Mossy arches lead down to four halls and a Warden. Its Vault Key points to a Sealed Vault full of treasure.
  • The Hidden School. An Explore Block opens onto islands in the sky. Four teachers there each ask a riddle and teach a gift.
  • Deeds and Trails. The Hall of Deeds tracks what you have done, with rewards, titles and ranks. Some deeds earn a glowing Trail.
🌾FarmingFields, bees and harvest festivals
  • Tilling. Use a Hoe on grass or dirt to make farmland.
  • Five crops. Wheat, ember fruit, lumen moss, rose hips and sun melons. You find their seeds in the wild plants of each realm.
  • Growing. A crop takes about two minutes to grow, and half that with water within four blocks. Crops grow faster in spring and summer.
  • Helping it along. Compost ripens a young crop on the spot, a Growth Bell rings crops ripe, and a Growth Sigil makes them grow three times as fast.
  • Helpers. A Farmer's Plot brings a farmer who plants and reaps. Allies and automatons can farm for you too.
  • Bigger harvests. The Farming skill and the Bloomfolk's knowledge give extra crops, and the Harvest Moon festival doubles them.
  • Locusts. Sometimes locusts come for your crops. Crops near a lantern or near you are safe.
📱The phone and helpA camera, a guide and someone to ask
  • The Smartphone. Make one at a Maker's Bench, then right click it or use Menu → 📱 Phone. Nine apps, from Camera to Factory.
  • Photo mode. Frame a shot or a selfie (a pet Lycheetah nearby hops in), add stickers and a caption, then share it or keep it in the 🖼 Gallery.
  • Lychee AI. Ask how to make something, where a creature lives or how to beat it. It answers offline from the game's own knowledge.
  • Five answers. Every item says why you want it, what it needs, how to use it, what it connects to and what it leads to.
  • The Field Guide. Press B and pick a goal under "I want to…". It finds your next step and lets you craft it on the spot.
  • Map and marks. M opens the map: tap to set waypoints. L jumps to a view of the realm's landmarks.
  • ⚡ Actions. One button opens every key as a button: roll, spells, ride, home, quests, camera, map and more.
👥Playing togetherOne shared world, across the room or the world
  • Room codes. Press P and open a room to get a six-letter code. A friend anywhere types it under Join with a room code and is in.
  • Join links. A link with your room code in it joins as soon as the world is up.
  • Invite codes. You can also host from your device by sending an invite code and pasting back the reply code.
  • One shared world. Every block anyone breaks or places appears for everyone. A friend who arrives gets the whole world.
  • See each other. Bodies, armour, tools, horses, Skywings and crafts, with a name over every head. Press Enter to chat.
  • Together on adventures. Share the Clockwork Reach and Neon City, warp to a friend, and show off your Ascension colours next to your name.
🎵Music and soundSongs the game composes as it plays
  • Realm themes. Each realm has its own music, which takes turns with eight more themes. Combat music rises when something hunts you.
  • The Jukebox. Build one and pick a theme. Every song is composed by the game as it plays.
  • Instruments. Play a Lute, Flute or Hand Drum. Look up or down to change the note.
  • The soundscape. Wind rises with height, surf swells by the sea, birds sing at dawn, and rooms echo indoors.
  • Note Blocks. Tune them and wire them to signals or a Sequencer to play tunes.
🎮Settings, devices and accessPC, phone, controller and TV
  • Starts light and quiet. The first launch is muted and on the lightest graphics. Nothing changes your settings behind your back.
  • Seven Settings tabs. Graphics, Sound, Camera, Game, Controls, Access and Worlds. Each opens with a line that says how things are set now.
  • Graphics. Five levels from Low to Max, Quick setup presets, view distance, leaves, sway and colour styles.
  • Speed test. Menu → ⏱ Speed test flies a 24-second loop. It tells you how your device runs the game and what to change.
