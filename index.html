The armoury

Sets that each do one thing their own way

A Mirror Knight who sends a quarter of every hit back. Thunderhide that calls lightning on whoever strikes you. Remade, piece by piece

Armour drawn whole, not painted on

Every set is built out of its own parts: cobalt cut in facets like its ore, a deep delver's lantern helm, infernal plate with molten cracks that glow, crystal lattice in a platinum grid, a hazmat suit with its back tank, a powered exo frame, a sunburst on Solar gold, suits for the sky and the Moon, a miner's lamp, a raider's war paint and two crowns.

Choose your road

Five paths, each to its capstone

After the first days the road splits five ways, and each path is a tree of steps you can see. Every step is one a player can take: a test bot walks them from a fresh home, the way you would, and it walks Machines, Farming and Hunting end to end.

⚙ MachinesA water wheel to a reactor: gearboxes, charge, steam, sorting, silicon and logic chips. 20 steps.
🌾 FarmingSeeds to a Harvest Feast: bees, herds, a Rain Tower, golden prize crops. 17 steps.
🏹 HuntingA sling to a Fierce foe: the Lens, the Lodge's daily bounty, trophies on the wall. 15 steps.
✨ MagicThe Lens to the Great Work: a school's riddle, spells of your own, potions, sigils. 11 steps.
🧭 AdventurePlaces in the wild, raider camps, the seven chapters of the Seal, other realms, the Moon. 28 steps.
Better together

A ring for friends

Open a room and your friends join from their own browsers, no account needed. Fight the Arena's waves side by side, each wave waiting for the friend still fighting theirs, and the room's board keeps everyone's best wave and the best party. Build Battles are there to share too.

The Forge Day

Dress up, drive off, play a song

Build an Armoury Hall and fill it with costumes: a giant Lychee Mascot, a Pirate Captain, a Moon Diver in a fishbowl helmet, and the Tailor of the Realms who comes at night to cut you something new. Dye any of them any colour. Park a Roadster, a Pirate Sloop or a Lychee Airship out front. Play the Band's songs to heal your pets, run like the wind or hit harder, and wrap a Gift Box for a friend.

No download, no install

From here to your first night

  1. Type your code at the top and press Play. The game opens in this tab.
  2. Start in Survival. You wake by a dying fire, and First Light walks you through your first night.
  3. Follow the Lycheetah's nose. It smells the Bloom a few minutes from home and puts it on your compass.
  4. Stuck? Press B for the Field Guide, or ask Lychee AI on the in-game phone.
