# ⚒ Lycheetah Realms

**Version 11.31** · updated 2026-10-18 · © Lycheetah, all rights reserved

A hand-forged voxel adventure across seven realms. Build, fight with fast, readable moves, glide and fly, raise towns into kingdoms, and chase the PILLAR on the horizon, with the Lycheetah at your side.

## Play

1. Open **https://lycheetah.github.io/PlayLYCrealms/**
2. Type the play code from Lycheetah's launch post on X, [@lycheetahlyc](https://x.com/lycheetahlyc). The device remembers it.
3. The first launch is silent and light. Tap 🔊 under Play for sound, and use Settings → Quick setup to raise the graphics.

It runs in a modern browser on **PC**, **phone and tablet**, and **TV and console browsers** (add `?console=1` to the address if the game won't start).

## Your first hour

- **Start in Survival** in a new world. A camp is waiting with a bedroll, a bench and a chest, and **First Light** (top left) walks you through eight steps: cut logs, make planks, build a bench, craft tools, and survive the first night.
- **Press B** for the handbook, and **E** for your bag and crafting (the 📜 Guide tab shows what to do next).
- **Night is dangerous.** Stay near the Hearth, keep torches lit, and greet the dawn.
- Lost? Ask **Lychee AI** on the in-game phone (make a Smartphone at a Maker's Bench), or type **/guide** after pressing Enter.

## The realms

Seven alchemical realms, each with its own land, light, creatures and riches, and more beyond them:

- **Mundus**, the middle realm: rivers, pasture, pine forests and peaks. Home, and where the road to the PILLAR begins.
- **Nigredo**: ash, ember and lava under a stone sky.
- **Albedo**: islands in the clouds. Mind the drop.
- **Rubedo**: red savanna, lychee groves and crimson mesas.
- **Viriditas**: fey glades, giant trees and glowing mushrooms.
- **Citrinitas**: golden haze, sun dunes and the pyramids of the old kings.
- **Mortificatio**, the Underdeep: bone fields and the Titan Graves, where titans walk and Grave Borers hunt from under the ground.
- **Beyond them:** time portals to Ancient Egypt, the Modern Day and the Far Future, plus Albedo's Moon, the Aurum, the Underworlds, the Rift, Neon City, the Hidden School and the Clockwork Reach.

## What you can do

- **Build** anything, block by block. Or lay down **kits**: whole homes, labs, farms, ships and landmarks, built from the blocks in your bag.
- **Learn the machines.** Creative → Kit Library → 🎓 Lessons builds working setups with a checklist. Water wheels, gearboxes, mills, furnaces, charge, signals and tubes all grow into automated factories. Every machine tells you in one line why it has stopped.
- **Fight** with combos, dashes, launchers and lock-on. Read each family's warning before it strikes, and face the guardians of every realm.
- **Travel:** glide, ride horses, call a Skywing, fly crafts you build yourself, and use waystones and the travel map.
- **Rule:** raise towns into kingdoms and trade between them.
- **The phone:** a camera with selfies and stickers, a Factory app that finds your stuck machines, a map, the weather, and Lychee AI.
- **Creative mode:** the whole bag, flight, Full Sets (a complete armour set and weapon in one tap), and every kit for free.

## If it runs slowly

The game starts on the lightest graphics, and nothing changes them behind your back. Raise them in **Settings → Graphics**. If it stutters, lower the **view distance** first. **Menu → ⏱ Speed test** measures your device.

## Found a bug?

This is an early build of a big game, so you will find rough edges. Please tell us: **Menu → ✉ Feedback → Copy report** puts your device and settings into one message to paste to Lycheetah.

## What's new

- **🔧 Every break fixed:** We read through four whole parts of the game, farming, vehicles, exploration and space, and fixed everything we found broken. Crops planted by a Farmer's Plot, the Seed Cannon, a farm kit or a sky garden now grow (they never did, and old ones near you will start); the Seed Cannon plants the seeds it fires, not always wheat; apiaries count every flower; and every fish you catch can be cooked. Aboard a craft, Y opens the Shipwright again (on foot it is still the Travel Map). Microchips and Plasma Cores can be made, so can the Jump Drive to the Moon; so can the Star Chart. Finishing the Road to the PILLAR gives you the title Pillar-Called at last, opened vaults and cleared Delves leave your compass, and the Hermit points at real raiders, not at his own hut.
- **🐌 The thin realms come alive:** Three realms had almost no life of their own, and now each has new creatures that belong there. On Albedo's white isles: Pearl Snails that leave a trail of light, Salt Hares with ears that glow, and Glass Herons on legs like blue glass. On the Moon: Crater Hoppers that bound sky-high in the low gravity, Grey Grazers with ice on their backs (calm, unless you crowd them), and Wreck Mites, brass beetles that guard the scrap where the sky-rail came down. In Neon City: Chrome Pigeons, and the Vend Crab, which lives in a little vending machine and is always out of order. Ask Lychee AI where to find them.
- **🪞 The Mirror Well:** Out on the white isles of Albedo, a still grove keeps a well whose surface is a mirror. Stand on it and it tells you the day the Aurum broke, twice, and the two tellings do not agree: one says something was born, the other says something was buried. Believe one by walking through its arch. The other arch crumbles for good, and the Well gives you the gift your belief earns: a Mirror Shard whose flash freezes whatever stands in front of you, or a Hollow Mirror you can step through to come out ten blocks on, even through a wall. The Well does not say if you were right. It is the third Myth Site.
- **😈 The Laughing Maw:** Once the Lycheetah Bloom has opened, it whispers of something further out in Mundus: a giant stone face half-buried among the wreck of a carnival, that has not laughed since the world broke. Stand on its tongue and give it three different silly things (it keeps one for you; find or make the others: a whoopee cushion is only wool and a hide). Give it something that is not funny and it will spit you across the field. Make it laugh, and a tooth falls out: the Maw Tooth, which makes every creature near you fall over laughing so you can slip away. Also: the Bloom now appears for everyone, not only after First Light, and its lychee tree has a ladder all the way up.
- **🌸 The Lycheetah Bloom:** Somewhere in Mundus, a few minutes from where your world began, there is a crater the broken world filled with something sweet: a meadow of lychee red and cheetah gold, a pool of light, and a giant flower that wears cheetah spots. It is closed. It is waiting. When you have found your feet, the Lycheetah will smell it and your compass will show the way. Find its three seeds and see what it does (it is a little ticklish). Its gift is a Bloom Seed: plant it at home and a little oasis grows that heals you, and a lychling comes to live there. It is the first of the Myth Sites: places built by hand from the Lycheetah canon. More are coming. Also: the Moonsilver Boots can be made again.
- **🐆 Lycheetah Realms:** The game has its real name: Lycheetah Realms. The title screen strikes it letter by letter, and the site, the phone and Feedback carry it too. Nothing else changes: every world, build and setting you have is right where you left it. Also: the Solar armour set can be finished at last. The Solar Helm and Solar Boots had lost their recipes (another set had taken their names), and both are back at the bench, 5 and 4 Solar Ingots.
