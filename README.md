# ⚒ Lycheetah Realms

**Version 11.47** · updated 2026-10-19 · © Lycheetah, all rights reserved

A hand-forged voxel adventure across seven realms. Build, fight with fast, readable moves, glide and fly, raise towns into kingdoms, and chase the PILLAR on the horizon, with the Lycheetah at your side.

## Play

1. Open **https://lycheetah.github.io/PlayLYCrealms/**
2. Type the play code from Lycheetah's launch post on X, [@lycheetahlyc](https://x.com/lycheetahlyc). The device remembers it.
3. The first launch is silent and light. Tap 🔊 under Play for sound, and use Settings → Quick setup to raise the graphics.

It runs in a modern browser on **PC**, **phone and tablet**, and **TV and console browsers** (add `?console=1` to the address if the game won't start).

## Your first hour

- **Start in Survival** in a new world. A camp is waiting with a bedroll, a bench and a chest, and **First Light** (top left) walks you through eight steps: cut logs, make planks, build a bench, craft tools, and survive the first night.
- **Press B** for the handbook, and **E** for your bag and crafting (the 📜 Guide tab shows what to do next).
- **Night is dangerous.** Stay near the Hearth, keep torches lit, and greet the dawn.
- Lost? Ask **Lychee AI** on the in-game phone (make a Smartphone at a Maker's Bench), or type **/guide** after pressing Enter.

## The realms

Seven alchemical realms, each with its own land, light, creatures and riches, and more beyond them:

- **Mundus**, the middle realm: rivers, pasture, pine forests and peaks. Home, and where the road to the PILLAR begins.
- **Nigredo**: ash, ember and lava under a stone sky.
- **Albedo**: islands in the clouds. Mind the drop.
- **Rubedo**: red savanna, lychee groves and crimson mesas.
- **Viriditas**: fey glades, giant trees and glowing mushrooms.
- **Citrinitas**: golden haze, sun dunes and the pyramids of the old kings.
- **Mortificatio**, the Underdeep: bone fields and the Titan Graves, where titans walk and Grave Borers hunt from under the ground.
- **Beyond them:** time portals to Ancient Egypt, the Modern Day and the Far Future, plus Albedo's Moon, the Aurum, the Underworlds, the Rift, Neon City, the Hidden School and the Clockwork Reach.

## What you can do

- **Build** anything, block by block. Or lay down **kits**: whole homes, labs, farms, ships and landmarks, built from the blocks in your bag.
- **Learn the machines.** Creative → Kit Library → 🎓 Lessons builds working setups with a checklist. Water wheels, gearboxes, mills, furnaces, charge, signals and tubes all grow into automated factories. Every machine tells you in one line why it has stopped.
- **Fight** with combos, dashes, launchers and lock-on. Read each family's warning before it strikes, and face the guardians of every realm.
- **Travel:** glide, ride horses, call a Skywing, fly crafts you build yourself, and use waystones and the travel map.
- **Rule:** raise towns into kingdoms and trade between them.
- **The phone:** a camera with selfies and stickers, a Factory app that finds your stuck machines, a map, the weather, and Lychee AI.
- **Creative mode:** the whole bag, flight, Full Sets (a complete armour set and weapon in one tap), and every kit for free.

## If it runs slowly

The game starts on the lightest graphics, and nothing changes them behind your back. Raise them in **Settings → Graphics**. If it stutters, lower the **view distance** first. **Menu → ⏱ Speed test** measures your device.

## Found a bug?

This is an early build of a big game, so you will find rough edges. Please tell us: **Menu → ✉ Feedback → Copy report** puts your device and settings into one message to paste to Lycheetah.

## What's new

- **🎩 The Velvet Syndicate:** A new crew in the realms, on the 🆕 New & Experimental shelf. The Velvet Syndicate suit: a fedora with a gold band, a pinstripe long coat with steel in the lining, pressed slacks and two-tone spats; wear all four and every foe that falls to you pays protection money, a gold ingot one time in three. Brass Knuckles punch as fast as claws. A Switchblade has the fastest swing there is. The Velvet Cane hides a blade: right click and you dash through every foe in your path. The Drum-Fed Chopper rattles off ten rounds a second while you hold right click. And the Armoured Briefcase, raised with right click held, cuts a hit from the front to a fifth. Nobody asks what is inside.
- **🗺️ Weathered Maps:** One find leads to the next. About one chest in six now holds a Weathered Map. Right click to read it: an X goes on your compass 200 to 420 blocks away. Walk to the X and you dig up a buried cache, richer than a chest and with gold in it, and two times in five there is another map inside. Dig up one for X Marks the Spot, and ten to be named Treasure Hunter.
- **🐆 The Lycheetah's Roads:** Every major forge is a road now, and the Lycheetah walks it with you. Open 🐆 The Roads in the Field Guide: eight roads (Space, Farming, Hunting, Machines, Magic, Combat, Exploration and Vehicles), five steps each, from a first try to that forge's showpiece: a base on the Moon, a Prize Harvest, a bounty claimed, the Endless Cell, the Philosopher's Stone, a legendary set, the Atlas, the Jump Drive. Every step pays a reward and the Lycheetah has something to say about it (it has opinions). Steps count whatever you have already done. Follow a road and its next step sits under your compass. Walk one to its end to be On the Road, and all eight to be The Lycheetah's Friend.
- **🏹 Hunting, a whole industry:** The hunt has a line now. Study a creature through the Lens and you strike it 10% harder: you know where it is weak. Tan hides into leather at the Press and make the Hunter's Leathers: all four pieces, and shy creatures let you come twice as close before they bolt. Smoke jerky at the Kiln for the long hunt. Build a Hunter's Lodge (Maker's Bench): every day it posts a bounty for three of a creature that lives in the realm you are in, keeps your record, and pays out in gold and leather. Study 25 creatures to be a Tracker, and claim ten bounties to be named Bounty Hunter. The Ranching page is now Hunting & Ranching.
- **✨ Magic, made real:** Magic is a loop now: see, learn, grow, brew, cast. The Lens teaches: the first time it shows you Ignis, Aqua, Vita, Aer or Umbra, your Spell Tome opens Ember, Frost, Life, Storm or Void, and every aspect you see is kept in your Codex (all twelve make you a Seer). With a wand in your bag, the Tome casts with Vis like your wands. And an Athanor is a brewing station: four potions from your own crops and Quintessence, each a magic effect for three minutes. Vis Draught (Lumen Moss) fills your Vis three times as fast, Thrift Tonic (Rose Hip) halves what spells cost, Warding Elixir (Ember Fruit) takes a quarter off every hit, and Mending Philter (Sun Melon and honey) heals a heart every two seconds. Potions sit beside meals, so you can have one of each.
- **⚙️ Machines that serve:** Your mill and your power now feed the rest of your world. The Millstone grinds wheat into flour, and flour bakes more Harvest Bread for less. A Harvester (Maker's Bench) set beside a turning Water Wheel, Windmill or Gearbox, with a chest beside it, picks the ripe crops around it, replants them and fills the chest. A Sprinkler fed by a running Water Pump needs no pond. The Press tans hides into Leather, and leather makes saddles. And the Athanor distils Lumen Moss into Quintessence and charges crystal into Vis Crystals, so your farm and your power feed your magic.
