# ⚒ Lycheetah Realms

**Version 11.37** · updated 2026-10-19 · © Lycheetah, all rights reserved

A hand-forged voxel adventure across seven realms. Build, fight with fast, readable moves, glide and fly, raise towns into kingdoms, and chase the PILLAR on the horizon, with the Lycheetah at your side.

## Play

1. Open **https://lycheetah.github.io/PlayLYCrealms/**
2. Type the play code from Lycheetah's launch post on X, [@lycheetahlyc](https://x.com/lycheetahlyc). The device remembers it.
3. The first launch is silent and light. Tap 🔊 under Play for sound, and use Settings → Quick setup to raise the graphics.

It runs in a modern browser on **PC**, **phone and tablet**, and **TV and console browsers** (add `?console=1` to the address if the game won't start).

## Your first hour

- **Start in Survival** in a new world. A camp is waiting with a bedroll, a bench and a chest, and **First Light** (top left) walks you through eight steps: cut logs, make planks, build a bench, craft tools, and survive the first night.
- **Press B** for the handbook, and **E** for your bag and crafting (the 📜 Guide tab shows what to do next).
- **Night is dangerous.** Stay near the Hearth, keep torches lit, and greet the dawn.
- Lost? Ask **Lychee AI** on the in-game phone (make a Smartphone at a Maker's Bench), or type **/guide** after pressing Enter.

## The realms

Seven alchemical realms, each with its own land, light, creatures and riches, and more beyond them:

- **Mundus**, the middle realm: rivers, pasture, pine forests and peaks. Home, and where the road to the PILLAR begins.
- **Nigredo**: ash, ember and lava under a stone sky.
- **Albedo**: islands in the clouds. Mind the drop.
- **Rubedo**: red savanna, lychee groves and crimson mesas.
- **Viriditas**: fey glades, giant trees and glowing mushrooms.
- **Citrinitas**: golden haze, sun dunes and the pyramids of the old kings.
- **Mortificatio**, the Underdeep: bone fields and the Titan Graves, where titans walk and Grave Borers hunt from under the ground.
- **Beyond them:** time portals to Ancient Egypt, the Modern Day and the Far Future, plus Albedo's Moon, the Aurum, the Underworlds, the Rift, Neon City, the Hidden School and the Clockwork Reach.

## What you can do

- **Build** anything, block by block. Or lay down **kits**: whole homes, labs, farms, ships and landmarks, built from the blocks in your bag.
- **Learn the machines.** Creative → Kit Library → 🎓 Lessons builds working setups with a checklist. Water wheels, gearboxes, mills, furnaces, charge, signals and tubes all grow into automated factories. Every machine tells you in one line why it has stopped.
- **Fight** with combos, dashes, launchers and lock-on. Read each family's warning before it strikes, and face the guardians of every realm.
- **Travel:** glide, ride horses, call a Skywing, fly crafts you build yourself, and use waystones and the travel map.
- **Rule:** raise towns into kingdoms and trade between them.
- **The phone:** a camera with selfies and stickers, a Factory app that finds your stuck machines, a map, the weather, and Lychee AI.
- **Creative mode:** the whole bag, flight, Full Sets (a complete armour set and weapon in one tap), and every kit for free.

## If it runs slowly

The game starts on the lightest graphics, and nothing changes them behind your back. Raise them in **Settings → Graphics**. If it stutters, lower the **view distance** first. **Menu → ⏱ Speed test** measures your device.

## Found a bug?

This is an early build of a big game, so you will find rough edges. Please tell us: **Menu → ✉ Feedback → Copy report** puts your device and settings into one message to paste to Lycheetah.

## What's new

- **🚀 Space, made whole:** The Moon is somewhere to live now, not just to visit. A Launch Gantry (Maker's Bench) shows your launch checklist and builds the space parts: a Pressure Suit makes your air last about twice as long. On the Moon, close a room on every side, Regolith Brick walls and a roof, with an Airlock in the wall, and it keeps its air: your O2 fills inside. Right click the Airlock to step through without letting the air out. An Ice Melter turns Moon Ice into air. A Gantry on the Moon forges Lunar Alloy, and Lunar Alloy makes the Vacuum Suit. Moon Meteorites now come home whole for the Star Forge (six starmetal each). Everything lives on one new 🚀 Space page in the Field Guide, with three deeds and the title Moonwright.
- **⚔️ Two new legendary sets:** The Selene Warden, forged from Moon Rock, Moon Ice and Selene Lily: the Moon's air lasts far longer, falls stop hurting, and the Lunar Hush throws back foes when you are low; its glaive, Moonfall, sends a frost-light shockwave and a soft leap. And the Maw Jester, from the Laughing Maw's teeth and bells: when a foe strikes you the bells laugh and every foe near you runs; its marotte blinks you behind a foe in a burst of confetti. Each set has a companion egg: the Moonling and the Grinling follow you between realms and fight beside you.
- **🧭 Exploration, made whole:** The map is yours to uncover: it stays dark until you have walked there, and the landmarks in the dark stay hidden. Hold the Spyglass and look at a landmark far off (out to 450 blocks): it is named, marked on your compass and uncovered on your map. Places now count when you walk into them, not when they appear near you, and the Atlas follows it: Cartographer for 150 squares of map, Far Sight for 10 landmarks spotted, and the title Atlas-Bearer for 50 places entered. The Field Guide's Exploration & Adventure page holds the whole line, with the Spyglass, the Star Chart and the Cartographer's Table.
- **🚗 Vehicles, made whole:** Everything you ride, drive, fly or sail now lives on one Field Guide page, Vehicles & Mounts, in five tiers: ride, wheels and rails, engines, sky, beyond. New: saddles. A Leather Saddle (four hides and an iron) makes every mount of that kind 15% faster; a Racing Saddle (a Leather Saddle, two gold and a feather) 30% faster, with a higher jump. Hold one and right click near your horse or any mount you have tamed. And the Garage (six iron, four planks and a circuit), the workshop for all of it: right click it to open the Shipwright, Shift + right click to call your craft to it.
- **🐉 Two new legendary sets:** The Hollowsun Dragonlord: obsidian scale veined with magma, four armour pieces, Sunfang the Dragonlord Greatsword (right click: Sunbreak, a 12 block wave of solar fire) and a Hollowsun Wyrmling Egg, forged from Sunscale and Wyrm Hearts. And the Glacial Sovereign: four armour pieces, Rimebane the Winter Runeblade and a Frost Colossus Egg, forged from Winter Hearts. Find them in Creative → 🆕 New & experimental, or forge them at the bench.
- **🌅 The sun put right:** Facing the sun used to wash the whole screen white: the shafts of sunlight took their light from the entire sky, and the glow lit anything bright, a blue sky included. Now the shafts come only from the sun, through the gaps in trees and hills, and the glow only from what is truly bright: the sun, lava, lamps. And the graphics levels mean something: High adds sun shadows with cool shaded sides, finer shafts and a softer glow, and sees 10 chunks; Ultra wider shadows and every light, 12 chunks; Max soft shadows and the finest light, 14 chunks. Choosing a level brings its view distance with it (you can still change it). Also: building a kit no longer refuses because a Delve or a road happens to be going up nearby.
