# ⚒ Lycheetah Realms

**Version 29.0** · updated 2026-10-24 · © Lycheetah, all rights reserved

A hand-forged voxel adventure across seven realms. Build, fight with fast, readable moves, glide and fly, raise towns into kingdoms, and chase the PILLAR on the horizon, with the Lycheetah at your side.

📘 **[The Book of Lycheetah Realms](GAME.md)**: the whole game, from the first fire to the Moon · 💬 **Join the Discord:** https://discord.gg/bS3METnJ5 · 🎬 **The trailer:** on the site, or [site/trailer.mp4](site/trailer.mp4)

## Play

1. Open **https://lycheetah.github.io/PlayLYCrealms/**
2. Type the play code from Lycheetah's launch post on X, [@lycheetahlyc](https://x.com/lycheetahlyc). The device remembers it.
3. The first launch is silent and light. Tap 🔊 under Play for sound, and use Settings → Quick setup to raise the graphics.

It runs in a modern browser on **PC**, **phone and tablet**, and **TV and console browsers** (add `?console=1` to the address if the game won't start).

## Your first hour

- **Start in Survival** in a new world. **The first days** (top left) walk you through fourteen steps: logs, planks and a bench, your first tools, food, shelter, the first night and iron. Then the road splits into five paths (Machines, Farming, Hunting, Magic, Adventure), each a tree of steps you can open with Shift+J.
- **Press B** for the handbook, and **E** for your bag and crafting (the 📜 Guide tab shows what to do next).
- **Night is dangerous.** Stay near the Hearth, keep torches lit, and greet the dawn.
- Lost? Ask **Lychee AI** on the in-game phone (make a Smartphone at a Maker's Bench), or type **/guide** after pressing Enter.

## The realms

Seven alchemical realms, each with its own land, light, creatures and riches, and more beyond them:

- **Mundus**, the middle realm: rivers, pasture, pine forests and peaks. Home, and where the road to the PILLAR begins.
- **Nigredo**: ash, ember and lava under a stone sky.
- **Albedo**: islands in the clouds. Mind the drop.
- **Rubedo**: red savanna, lychee groves and crimson mesas.
- **Viriditas**: fey glades, giant trees and glowing mushrooms.
- **Citrinitas**: golden haze, sun dunes and the pyramids of the old kings.
- **Mortificatio**, the Underdeep: bone fields and the Titan Graves, where titans walk and Grave Borers hunt from under the ground.
- **Beyond them:** time portals to Ancient Egypt, the Modern Day and the Far Future, plus Albedo's Moon, the Aurum, the Underworlds, the Rift, Neon City, the Hidden School and the Clockwork Reach.

## What you can do

- **Build** anything, block by block. Or lay down **kits**: whole homes, labs, farms, ships and landmarks, built from the blocks in your bag.
- **Learn the machines.** Creative → Kit Library → 🎓 Lessons builds working setups with a checklist. Water wheels, gearboxes, mills, furnaces, charge, signals and tubes all grow into automated factories. Every machine tells you in one line why it has stopped.
- **Fight** with combos, dashes, launchers and lock-on. Read each family's warning before it strikes, and face the guardians of every realm.
- **Travel:** glide, ride horses, call a Skywing, fly crafts you build yourself, and use waystones and the travel map.
- **Rule:** raise towns into kingdoms and trade between them.
- **The phone:** a camera with selfies and stickers, a Factory app that finds your stuck machines, a map, the weather, and Lychee AI.
- **Creative mode:** the whole bag, flight, Full Sets (a complete armour set and weapon in one tap), and every kit for free.

## If it runs slowly

The game starts on the lightest graphics, and nothing changes them behind your back. Raise them in **Settings → Graphics**. If it stutters, lower the **view distance** first. **Menu → ⏱ Speed test** measures your device.

## Found a bug?

This is an early build of a big game, so you will find rough edges. Please tell us: **Menu → ✉ Feedback → Copy report** puts your device and settings into one message to paste to Lycheetah.

## What's new

- **🌿 The realms breathe:** The air moves now, each realm its own way: dandelion seeds and fireflies in Mundus, embers in Nigredo, snow in Albedo, petals in Rubedo, pollen in Viriditas, golden glints in Citrinitas, ash and soul-wisps in Mortificatio. Leaves fall from the trees, and fish leap in the water.
- **☠ The loot lands:** Mortificatio is a realm you raid now: Grave Caches erupt in the wastes with a beam you can see from afar; come close and the dead rise to guard them. Clear the guard and the chest opens. Open them in a row and the frenzy climbs, common to rare to epic to legendary.
- **🎈 The zeppelins:** Every town and port now has a passenger zeppelin to its nearest neighbour. It comes down low over town with a rope ladder: hold Space at the ladder to climb aboard, then it rises into the high air and carries you there. Jump off whenever you like (with a glider, a fine way into the sky).
- **🚂 The dwarf trains:** The dwarves' railway runs now: a brass engine with a dwarf at the controls and two open cars on every line between Railholds, on a timetable. Step aboard at a station or as it passes and it carries you over the hills; jump off whenever you like.
- **🗿 The Colossus, together:** In a shared world everyone now fights the same Iron Colossus: it walks for all of you, its bar shows for all of you, every blow lands, its stomp and its shells come at whoever is near, and the killing blow's Core is yours.
- **🌅 The first minute:** The first days now point the way: under the gold pill a small pointer turns toward what the step needs (the nearest tree, the bedroll, the bench, bare stone, an animal) and says how far. And the title is calm: the tour and ▶ Play, then one row of small chips.
