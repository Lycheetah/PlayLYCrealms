## 1. What Lycheetah Realms is

Lycheetah Realms is a hand-forged voxel adventure that plays in your web browser, with nothing to download or install. You begin by a dying fire in Mundus, the middle realm, with the Lycheetah, a wild cat of the red canyons, watching from the long grass. From there the game opens out into seven alchemical realms, each with its own land, light, creatures and riches, and many more places beyond them.

It is a survival and crafting game, but not only that. Around the building and the gathering it has:

- **places built by hand**, from myths of its own;
- **a story campaign** of seven chapters;
- **eight trees of progression**, each a tree of steps you can see;
- **machines** you watch at work;
- **an Arena** you can fight with friends;
- **a radio** with stations of its own music.

## 3. Getting started

1. Open **https://lycheetah.github.io/PlayLYCrealms/**.
2. Enter the play code from Lycheetah's launch post on X, [@lycheetahlyc](https://x.com/lycheetahlyc). Your device remembers it.
3. The first launch is quiet and light on your device. Tap 🔊 for sound, and use **Settings → Quick setup** to raise the graphics if your device can take it.

**Your first hour.**
- Start in **Survival** in a new world.
- **The first days**, fourteen steps shown at the top left, walk you through it: cut logs, make planks and a bench, craft your first tools, find food, build shelter, survive the first night and reach iron.
- When they end, the road splits five ways (section 5).

## 4. The world

### The seven realms

| Realm | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Mundus** | The middle realm: rivers, pasture, pine forests and peaks. Home, and where the road begins. |
| **Nigredo** | The Blackening: ash, ember and lava under a stone sky. |
| **Albedo** | Islands in the clouds. Mind the drop. |
| **Rubedo** | Red savanna, lychee groves and crimson mesas, where the Lycheetah runs wild. |
| **Viriditas** | Fey glades, giant trees and glowing mushrooms. |
| **Citrinitas** | Golden haze, sun dunes and the pyramids of the old kings. |
| **Mortificatio** | The Underdeep: bone fields and the Titan Graves, where titans walk and Grave Borers hunt from under the ground. |

### Beyond the seven

Time portals lead to Ancient Egypt, the Modern Day and the Far Future. A craft with a Jump Drive reaches the Moon and its base. There are also the Aurum, the Underworlds, the Rift, Neon City, the Hidden School and the Clockwork Reach.

### Places built by hand

The land is full of places to find: crashed airships, wizard towers, abandoned mines, lost temples, raider camps, old farmsteads, standing stones, bee groves and pedlars' outposts. Some are **Myth Sites**, hand-built from the game's own myths, like the Lycheetah Bloom, the Laughing Maw and the Mirror Well.

The map stays dark until you have walked there. A Spyglass names a landmark far off and marks it on your compass.

### Creatures

About 150 kinds, many you will meet nowhere else:
- Rubedo's Lycheetahs;
- the Old Titans asleep where the giants fell;
- Crater Hoppers in the Moon's low gravity;
- a crab that lives in a vending machine.

Some foes are **Fierce**: bigger, glittering gold and twice as tough. Look at any creature through the **Lens** to add it to your **Bestiary**.

## 5. The road: the first days and the eight trees

After the first days the road splits into **eight trees**: the five paths below, and the three that grew later (🪶 **The Sky**: a glider, the archipelagos and the Cloud Sea; 🦾 **Mechs**: a cockpit cage, legs and arms, to the Iron Colossus; ⚡ **Power**: every way to make power, from water to fusion). Each is a tree of steps you can open at any time (Shift+J), with what to do next always shown on screen. You can walk one path, or all of them.

| Path | From | To its capstone | Steps |
|---|---|---|---|
| ⚙ **Machines** | a water wheel and a gearbox | a reactor and the fusion engine, by way of charge, steam, sorting tubes, silicon and logic chips | 20 |
| 🌾 **Farming** | seeds and a hoe | the Harvest Feast, by way of bees, herds, a Rain Tower and golden prize crops | 17 |
| 🏹 **Hunting** | a sling and hide armour | felling a Fierce foe, by way of the Lens, the Hunter's Lodge and its daily bounty, and trophies | 15 |
| ✨ **Magic** | the Lens and an aspect seen | the Great Work, by way of a school's riddle, spells of your own, potions and sigils | 11 |
| 🧭 **Adventure** | places in the wild | the Moon, by way of raider camps, the Rift, a Guardian, a Delve, the seven chapters and other realms | 28 |

**Every step can be taken.** A test bot plays each path from a fresh home the way a player would, and walks Machines, Farming and Hunting end to end. Where a step needs another realm, the step's hint says so.

## 6. The story: the Lycheetah Seal

The campaign is the **Lycheetah Seal**, broken into seven fragments, each kept in a place of its own. Each chapter is opened with a seal you make at the bench, and each ends with a warden.

1. **The Hollow Pillar**
2. **The Sky Orrery**
3. **The Sunken Bell**
4. **The Ember Foundry**
5. **The Frozen Choir**
6. **The Storm Spire**
7. **The Heart of the Seal**, where the seventh fragment was made and all seven are brought together

Each chapter also has a **Trial**, a harder run for a medal.

## 7. Modes

From **Play a Mode** on the title screen:

| Mode | What it is |
|---|---|
| ⚔ **The Arena** | Waves that grow, gold between them, a merchant and champions every fifth wave. Play alone or **with friends**: a cleared wave waits for a friend still fighting theirs. |
| 🏰 **The Delve** | Floor after floor down a Rift, a boss every fifth, and a **Daily Delve** on the day's seed, the same for everyone. |
| 🏃 **Parkour Trials** | Hand-built courses for the dash, the wall hop and the glide, timed, with gold, silver and bronze. |
| 🧱 **Build Battle** | A theme, a timer and a plot; judged on what you built, and playable with friends. |
| 🌋 **Challenge Worlds** | Six worlds with rules of their own, from Hardcore (one life) to the Gentle Road. |
| 🏝 **One Island** | A whole game on a single island. |

## 8. Systems in depth

### Building and kits
- Build anything, block by block.
- Or lay down **kits**: whole homes, labs, farms, ships and landmarks, built from the blocks in your bag.
- In Creative, the Kit Library's lessons build working machine setups with a checklist.

### Machines and power
- Two kinds of power:
  - **Turning power** from water wheels, windmills and steam engines, carried by gearboxes;
  - **Charge** from generators, carried by cable.
- Machines **evolve** into better ones (a Millstone into a Crusher, an Electric Furnace into an Arc Furnace) and keep what is inside them.
- Every machine says in one line why it has stopped.
- A Power Meter shows what your network makes, needs, stores and loses.

### Farming and creatures
- Crops grow faster when watered.
- Bees make honey beside flowers.
- Tame a creature by feeding it what it likes; a herd at a Ranch Post leaves the day's produce.
- A companion fights beside you, and bites three times as hard inside the Seal's chapters.

### Hunting
- The **Lens** studies a creature, and studied creatures take more harm.
- The **Hunter's Lodge** posts a bounty each day for a creature of your realm. It stays on screen until you claim it.

### Magic
- See an aspect through the Lens.
- Find a school and answer its riddle.
- Learn from a teacher and cast with a wand.
- Inscribe spells of your own in the Spell Tome.
- Brew potions at an Athanor, and charge sigils (growth, ward, travel).

### Combat
- Combos, dashes, launchers and lock-on.
- Each family of foe warns before it strikes.
- Difficulty runs from peaceful to brutal.
- The Combat skill and a Hunter's Mark make your blows land harder.

### Armour and gear
- More than fifty sets, each drawn whole on your body from its own parts, not painted on, and each doing one thing its own way:
  - a Mirror Knight sends back a quarter of every hit;
  - Thunderhide calls lightning;
  - Skyweave gives a second jump.
- Show sets off on an Armour Stand at home.

### Travel
- Glide, ride horses and mounts.
- Call a Skywing.
- Fly crafts you build yourself.
- Use waystones and the travel map.

### Music and the radio
- A radio of eight stations, Lycheetah Realms FM among them, with 45 songs written for the game.
- Each realm has its own theme.
- Jukeboxes play any of them.

### Deeds and ranks
- The **Hall of Deeds** keeps over 560 deeds, grouped by tree.
- Deeds raise your rank, and some give a title to wear.

## 9. Playing together

Press **P** to open a room. Friends join from their own browsers with an invite code, with no accounts and nothing to install. In a room you can:

- chat;
- fight the Arena's waves together;
- keep the room's board of best waves and best parties;
- hold a Build Battle.

## 10. Platforms, settings and accessibility

- **PC:** keyboard and mouse, or a controller.
- **Phones and tablets:** full touch controls, with menus laid out for a phone.
- **TV and console browsers:** add `?console=1` to the address if the game will not start.
- **Settings:**
  - graphics quality, view distance and resolution, with an automatic mode;
  - text size;
  - sound and music levels;
  - controller remapping;
  - a Quick setup that picks sensible settings for your device.
- If the game ever struggles, it says so, saves your world and rests the music for a minute.

## 11. Saves and your data

- Your worlds are saved in your own browser, on your own device. There is no account and no server holding your world.
- **Menu → 💾 Back up this world** saves a copy you can keep.
- Clearing your browser's site data clears your worlds, so back up the ones you love.

## 13. How the game is made

Lycheetah Realms is designed and directed by Lycheetah and forged by LY-Athanor, an AI builder, working from Lycheetah's calls.

Every update goes through a gate of about 300 automatic checks, playing the real game in a browser, before it is published. Path bots play each path from home to prove its steps can be taken.

The game is written from scratch: its blocks and pictures are drawn in code, and its music is composed for it.
